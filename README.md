# ClashPerk

**The Clash of Clans bot for Discord.** ClashPerk is used in more than 40,000 Discord servers and has been tracking clans since 2019. It reads your clans and players from Supercell's official Clash of Clans API and posts wars, CWL, legend days, clan changes and reminders right in your server.

**[Add to Discord](https://clashperk.com/invite)** · [Website](https://clashperk.com) · [Setup guide](https://docs.clashperk.com/overview/getting-set-up) · [Dashboard](https://app.clashperk.com) · [Clash of Clans Wiki](https://wiki.clashperk.com) · [Support server](https://discord.gg/ppuppun)

## What it does

- **War and CWL logs:** every attack posted live with stars and destruction, round-by-round CWL rankings and season reports.
- **Reminders:** ping members before the war ends, with a log of every missed attack.
- **Clan logs:** member joins and leaves, role changes, Town Hall upgrades and donation summaries.
- **Legend League tracking:** daily attacks, defenses and net trophies, with legend logs and leaderboards.
- **Auto-roles:** roles by clan, Town Hall and league, with verified account links.
- **Rosters:** sign-ups for wars and CWL with Town Hall requirements, exportable to Google Sheets.
- **Player and clan lookups:** profiles, hero equipment, rushed bases, remaining upgrades and war history.
- **Web dashboard:** manage the same settings at [app.clashperk.com](https://app.clashperk.com).

## Get started

1. [Add ClashPerk](https://clashperk.com/invite) to your server. You need the Manage Server permission there.
2. Link your clan with its tag.
3. Turn on the logs and reminders you want, in Discord or on the dashboard.

The [setup guide](https://docs.clashperk.com/overview/getting-set-up) walks through every step, and the [documentation](https://docs.clashperk.com) covers every command (sponsored by [GitBook](https://www.gitbook.com/)).

## Is it safe for my account?

Yes. ClashPerk only reads public data from Supercell's official Clash of Clans API. It never signs in to the game or plays for you.
